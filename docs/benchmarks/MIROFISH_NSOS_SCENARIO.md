# MIROFISH-BENCHMARK-001 — NSOS Synthetic Scenario

## Scenario

A fictional Nigerian secondary school introduces NSOS as its central digital operating platform.

The announcement:

> Parents can access fees, attendance, results, communication, and learning resources through one digital platform.

## Synthetic population

Create configurable agents representing:

- students
- parents
- teachers
- principals
- proprietors
- school administrators
- education regulators
- education vendors
- alumni
- community members
- education influencers

All identities must be synthetic. Do not use real people's personal data.

## Baseline conditions

Agents receive:
- role
- age band where relevant
- fictional school relationship
- baseline digital-literacy level
- communication preference
- initial awareness
- initial trust
- constraints
- goals
- social connections

These are simulation parameters, not claims about Nigerian populations.

## Intervention A — minimal launch

NSOS launches without structured onboarding.

## Intervention B — supported launch

NSOS launches with:
- WhatsApp onboarding
- teacher training
- parent tutorials
- simple support escalation
- staged feature introduction

## Simulation windows

Run:
- 72 simulated hours
- 7 simulated days
- 30 simulated days

Use the same seed, population generation procedure, model configuration, and randomization controls for paired A/B runs wherever technically possible.

## Metrics

Measure:
- awareness
- attempted adoption
- successful adoption
- support demand
- teacher participation
- parent engagement
- administrative workload
- resistance events
- misinformation events
- feature discovery
- abandonment
- intervention sensitivity

## Interpretation rule

Outputs represent behavior generated by the simulation under its assumptions. They are not empirical forecasts of Nigerian schools or parents.

The benchmark is testing whether OAE can construct, execute, instrument, compare, and interrogate the simulation—not whether the simulation has predictive validity in the real world.
