The public [DataFactor data-partnership page](https://datafactor.com/data-partner) clarifies that it is a data-licensing inquiry, not a repository-rating service. It presents a contact form and says that submitting it does not authorize data access or tool connection; it also states that scope, legal terms, and security requirements require company approval. The page’s public FAQ specifically asks whether AI organizations receive raw repositories or source code, reinforcing that any repository-related disclosure belongs to a later, scoped partnership process rather than a self-service scoring workflow.

## Boundary

This assessment is not a negative rating of OAE or DataFactor. It only records the absence of a discovered self-service repository-rating path on the reviewed public page. A DataFactor partnership inquiry could require code, metadata, contact details, or commercial information. Those are external disclosures and require a separate, explicit user confirmation.
